CHROTE / Gas City Workbench

A compact map of the decisions required before CHROTE can treat Gas City as reliable orchestration plumbing.

North star

  • Gas City is orchestration plumbing: identities, mail, sling, workflows, molecules, and events.
  • CHROTE is the access layer for named tmux and Gas City identities.
  • No hidden mocks. Unsupported paths fail loud.
  • Beads remain the work ledger. Context Citadel remains durable context, not orchestration.

Working set

Required Decisions

The first-pass answer is not "build more UI." It is to choose the access-layer boundaries and failure semantics that keep Gas City real, recoverable, and useful for agent collaboration.

Immediate pressure CHROTE must serve the shell-first source and expose only intended identities.
Architecture pressure The HTTP/CLI split and tmux assumptions need an explicit transport decision.
Durability pressure The live city config must either be reproducible or explicitly disposable.
